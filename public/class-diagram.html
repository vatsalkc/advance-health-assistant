Classes in the System:
  • User: id, name, email, phone, age, gender
  • Doctor: id, name, email, specialization, experience, rating
  • Appointment: id, userId, doctorId, date, time, status
  • SymptomCheck: id, userId, symptoms, disease, confidence
  • Medicine: id, userId, name, dosage, time

Relationships: User has many Appointments, SymptomChecks, and Medicines. Doctor has many Appointments.
