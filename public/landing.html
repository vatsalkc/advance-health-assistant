About the Project

Comprehensive healthcare solution powered by AI

Problem Statement

Healthcare accessibility remains a critical challenge with long waiting times, difficulty finding specialists, and lack of immediate health guidance. Patients often delay seeking medical help due to uncertainty, leading to 30% of unnecessary emergency visits.

  • 60% of patients delay medical help due to uncertainty
  • 40% medication non-compliance due to forgetfulness
  • 2-3 weeks average wait for specialist appointments
  • Fragmented medical records across providers

Our Solution

Advanced Health Assistant provides a comprehensive digital healthcare platform that bridges the gap between patients and healthcare providers through AI-powered features.

  • AI Symptom Checker: 34 diseases, 16 specializations
  • Smart Appointments: Real-time booking & tracking
  • Medicine Reminders: Automated notifications
  • Doctor Portal: Complete practice management
  • 24/7 AI Chatbot: Instant health support

Technology Stack

Frontend: React.js, Bootstrap 5
Backend: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
AI: Google Gemini, Custom ML
Auth: Supabase Auth with RLS

Key Metrics

Database: 7 tables, 85+ columns
Coverage: 34 diseases, 16 specializations
Doctors: 36+ pre-registered
Security: 30+ RLS policies

Impact

Accessibility: 24/7 health support
Efficiency: Instant symptom analysis
Compliance: Better medication adherence
Records: Centralized health data
