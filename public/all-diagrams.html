Relationships:
  • User (abstract) is inherited by Patient, Doctor, and Admin
  • Patient manages Medicine (1 to many)
  • Patient books Appointment (1 to many)
  • Doctor attends Appointment (1 to many)
  • Admin maintains SymptomDataset (1 to 1)
  • MLEngine processes SymptomDataset (dashed dependency)
