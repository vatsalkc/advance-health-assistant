# Patient Details Page Restructure - Complete Summary

## Overview
Successfully restructured the patient details page with a new tab organization and dedicated "My Diagnoses" section that prominently displays all diagnoses written by the doctor for each patient.

## New Tab Structure

### 1. **Overview Tab** (Default)
- **Quick Summary**: Recent activity and health statistics
- **Recent Appointments**: Last 3 appointments with status indicators
- **Health Metrics**: Total visits, diagnoses given, prescriptions, symptom checks
- **Latest Diagnosis**: Shows the most recent diagnosis provided by the doctor
- **Visual Indicators**: Clear badges showing diagnosis and prescription status

### 2. **My Diagnoses Tab** ⭐ (NEW - Primary Feature)
- **Dedicated Section**: Shows ONLY diagnoses written by the current doctor
- **Chronological Order**: Most recent diagnoses first
- **Numbered Diagnoses**: Each diagnosis is numbered for easy reference
- **Complete Context**: Shows patient complaint, doctor's diagnosis, and prescribed treatment
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Clear distinction between patient complaint and doctor's diagnosis
- **Time Stamps**: Shows when each diagnosis was made with "days ago" format
- **Enhanced Styling**: Special styling to highlight doctor's diagnoses

### 3. **All Appointments Tab**
- **Complete History**: All appointments regardless of status
- **Enhanced Display**: Better visual organization with status-based color coding
- **Diagnosis Highlighting**: Doctor's diagnoses are prominently displayed with special styling
- **Prescription Integration**: Shows prescriptions alongside diagnoses

### 4. **Prescriptions Tab**
- **Prescription Focus**: Shows only appointments where prescriptions were given
- **Associated Diagnoses**: Links prescriptions with their corresponding diagnoses
- **Treatment History**: Complete prescription history for the patient

### 5. **Patient History Tab**
- **Combined View**: Symptom history and medical reports in side-by-side layout
- **Chronological Order**: Most recent items first
- **Comprehensive Overview**: All patient-generated data and external reports

## Key Features of "My Diagnoses" Tab
