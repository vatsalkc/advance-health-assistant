WHY DO I NEED TO REGISTER?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The sample doctors in DOCTOR_CREDENTIALS.txt are just templates.
They don't have authentication accounts yet.

Registration creates:
✅ Authentication account (email + password)
✅ Links it to doctor profile
✅ Enables login functionality

Think of it like this:
- Sample data = Doctor's business card
- Registration = Creating their login account

TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "Invalid email or password"
   → Doctor not registered yet. Register first!

❌ "This email is already registered"
   → Good! Just login with the credentials

❌ "Doctor profile not found"
   → You're on patient login. Use "Login as Doctor"

❌ Can't find "Login as Doctor" link
   → Look at the bottom of the login page
